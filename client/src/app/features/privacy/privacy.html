Privacy

What this site keeps, and what it does not.

Last updated {{ updated }}

the maestro is a personal archive of one writer's work, free to read. There are no accounts for readers, no advertising, no analytics services and no tracking across other sites. Nothing here is sold or shared with anyone.

What is stored about readers

  • An anonymous reader id. A random value your browser makes the first time you visit, kept in its local storage. It lets your emote reactions be counted once and undone, and lets the author see how many people are reading. It is not linked to your name, email, IP address or device.
  • Page visits. Which page of this site was opened, when, for roughly how long, the page reached in a book, and a broad category for how you arrived (Instagram, Google, direct, or other). The referring address itself is not kept.
  • Preferences on your device only. Your light or dark theme, and whether you chose "Never show again" on the message on the home page. These stay in your browser and are never sent to the server.

The message on the home page

The home page shows a message meant for one person, opened by answering four questions. What you type into those questions is checked and not kept: the server stores only a one-way hash of the correct answers.

If you try it, the network (IP) address you tried from is recorded, with the time and whether the attempt worked, and the author can see that list. After a wrong attempt you may choose to give an email address so the author can send you a code; if you do, that address is emailed to the author and shown beside your attempt. These records are deleted after six months. Reading the rest of the site records none of this.

Email, and the author's Google account

When someone asks for a code to that message, the site emails the author the code and the address they gave. It sends through the Gmail API from the author's own Google account, with permission only to send email (gmail.send). The site does not read, store or share any email, contact or other Google account data, and does not use it for anything but sending those notes to the author. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Services the site runs on

The site is hosted on Vercel and Render, its database is MongoDB Atlas, and images, books and recordings are served from Cloudflare R2. Like any web host, these see the network requests that reach them. Video thumbnails are loaded from YouTube, and a video player is loaded from YouTube only when you press play. The message screen loads one typeface from Google Fonts.

Cookies

Readers get none. The only cookie is a sign-in cookie for the author.

Questions, or removing something

Write to {{ contact }}. Your anonymous reader id can be erased by clearing this site's data in your browser.
